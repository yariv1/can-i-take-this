With limits

Some everyday medicines are controlled or banned in Australia. Check the ones you carry below.

  • Always carry medication in its original packaging with your prescription or a doctor's letter.
  • Bring only a personal supply (usually up to ~30–90 days).
  • The rule that matters is the destination's — not your home country's.
