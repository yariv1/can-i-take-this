Can I bring Ritalin into Kuwait? (2026)

With limits

Allowed only if you arrange approval first.

Ritalin contains Methylphenidate (also sold as Ritalin, Concerta). Carry it in original packaging with your prescription or a doctor's letter.

  • Methylphenidate is controlled in Kuwait - you need an import permit or advance approval before you travel.
  • Apply ahead of time and carry the approval together with your prescription.

Source: Kuwait health/customs authority
