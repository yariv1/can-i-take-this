Duty-free alcohol allowance for Luxembourg (2026)

With limits

Within your duty-free allowance.

  • From outside the EU: 1 L spirits (over 22% ABV) OR 2 L fortified/sparkling wine, PLUS 4 L still wine and 16 L beer, per adult. No limits travelling between EU countries (personal use).
  • Over the allowance, you pay duty at customs.

Source: Luxembourg customs authority
