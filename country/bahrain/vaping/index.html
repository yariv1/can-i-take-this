❓ Quick answers

Is vaping legal in Bahrain?

WHO's 2023 country profile for Bahrain (data as at 2022) lists no general ban on e-cigarettes and says national laws or regulations regulate them. WHO reports no national ban on using e-cigarettes in indoor public places, workplaces and public transport in Bahrain. That is the law on e-cigarette products: it does not say how many devices or how much e-liquid customs lets you carry in a bag, so check with the customs authority before you travel.

Can I bring a vape to Bahrain?

WHO lists no general ban on e-cigarettes in Bahrain, but its profile does not publish a traveller allowance. Check the customs rules for Bahrain, and on the plane keep the device in your cabin baggage as airlines require.

Can I vape in public places in Bahrain?

WHO reports no national ban on using e-cigarettes in indoor public places, workplaces and public transport in Bahrain.

What is the minimum age to buy a vape in Bahrain?

WHO reports a minimum age of sale of 18 for e-cigarettes in Bahrain.
