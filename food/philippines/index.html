Some foods are fine, others are restricted or destroyed on arrival in Philippines.

At the border
Category · Meat productsDeclare on arrival

Meat & cured meats is restricted — declare it.

  • Meat products is limited in Philippines and may be inspected or refused.
  • Declare it on arrival; sealed commercial products have the best chance.
Food rules protect against pests and disease. When unsure, always declare it - declaring is free, smuggling is fined.
