Can I bring food into France? (2026)

With limits

Some foods are fine, others are restricted or destroyed on arrival in France. See the breakdown below.

  • Sealed, commercially packaged food has the best chance of being accepted.
  • When in doubt, declare it — declaring is free, smuggling is fined.
