Meat
🥩 RomaniaFOOD

Meat and meat products: allowed inside the EU, not allowed from outside it.

AllowedFrom another EU country
  • You can carry meat and meat products for your own personal consumption.
  • The same applies from Andorra, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, San Marino and Switzerland.
  • In a local animal-disease outbreak, restrictions can apply to the type and amount.
Not allowedFrom outside the EU
  • Travellers arriving from a non-EU country are not allowed to bring any meat or meat products.
  • Why: the pathogens that cause foot and mouth disease and classical swine fever can live in meat and be carried in baggage.
  • Exception: up to 10 kg from the Faroe Islands or Greenland.
  • Caught with it: the meat is seized and destroyed, and you can be liable for costs or penalties.

Dairy
🥩 RomaniaFOOD

Milk and dairy products follow the same rule as meat.

AllowedFrom another EU country
  • Milk, cheese and other dairy are allowed for your own personal consumption.
Not allowedFrom outside the EU
  • No milk or dairy products can be brought in.
  • Exception: up to 10 kg from the Faroe Islands or Greenland.
  • Exception: less than 2 kg of powdered infant milk, infant food or special foods needed for medical reasons, if they need no refrigeration, are a packaged proprietary brand and the packaging is unbroken.

❓ Quick answers

Can I bring meat or cheese into Romania from another EU country?

Yes, for your own personal consumption. The same applies to fruit and vegetables grown in an EU country and free from pests or disease.

Can I bring meat or dairy into Romania from outside the EU?

No. Travellers arriving from a non-EU country are not allowed to bring any meat or dairy products, apart from narrow exceptions such as less than 2 kg of powdered infant milk, baby food and special medical foods.

Can I bring honey or eggs into Romania from outside the EU?

A limited quantity of eggs, egg products and honey can be brought; the Commission lists up to 2 kg for other animal products such as honey.

How much fish can I bring into Romania from outside the EU?

Up to 20 kg of fishery products, or the weight of one fish if it weighs more.
