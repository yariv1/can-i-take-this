JetBlue Baggage Fees 2026: Peak vs Off-Peak Prices and Blue Basic Rules

Airline Fees · Updated October 2026 · 7 min read

JetBlue prices checked bags by the calendar: $45 for the first bag and $59 for the second on off-peak dates, $49 and $69 on peak dates, when you add them at least 24 hours before departure. A third bag is $200. Blue Basic fares include no checked bag.

📅 Why the price changes by date

JetBlue splits the year into off-peak and peak. Peak dates cover most of spring and summer plus the November and December holidays, so the cheapest bag price only applies on quieter dates. Check the date of your flight, not the date you booked.

These prices include the saving for adding the bag at least 24 hours before departure. JetBlue’s fee page shows higher prices if you add the bag later or at the airport, so add it when you book or before the 24-hour mark.

🔵 Blue Basic: what is and is not included

  • No checked bag is included on Blue Basic, so every bag follows the table above.
  • One free carry-on bag and a personal item are included since September 6, 2024.
  • Main, Main Flex and the EvenMore fares do not include a checked bag either on domestic routes, so the table applies to them too.

🆓 Who skips the fee

  • Mint: first and second checked bags are included.
  • Mosaic members: the first bag is included from Mosaic 2, and the second bag is included for higher Mosaic tiers.
  • JetBlue cardmembers: the first checked bag is included.

Rules change by tier and fare, so open your reservation on jetblue.com to see which bags are already covered.

  • Two bags on an off-peak date: $45 + $59 = $104.

❓ Quick answers

How much is the first checked bag on JetBlue?

$45 on off-peak dates and $49 on peak dates for domestic, Caribbean, Latin America and Canada flights, when added at least 24 hours before departure.

What counts as a peak date on JetBlue?

JetBlue publishes a peak calendar on its fee page that includes most of spring and summer and the November and December holidays. Your travel date decides it.

Does Blue Basic include a checked bag?

No. It includes one free carry-on and a personal item, and you pay for any checked bag.

How much is an overweight bag on JetBlue?

$150 for a bag from 51 to 99 lb. A bag that is also oversize pays both charges.

Is the third bag $200?

Yes on domestic, Caribbean, Latin America and Canada flights. On transatlantic flights it is $200, £150 or €185 off-peak.

Before you fly — checklist

1
Check whether your flight date is peak or off-peak on jetblue.com.
2

4

5
Add your Mosaic number or JetBlue card to the booking.
