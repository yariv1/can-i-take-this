Duty-Free Tobacco Allowance by Country (2026): Cigarettes, Cigars & Heated Tobacco

Customs & Money · Updated 2026 · 9 min read

The number on the duty-free sign is not the number that matters. The tobacco allowance is set by the country you are arriving in, and it ranges from 600 cigarettes in Turkey to none at all in Singapore. The usual figure is 200 — one carton — but a long list of countries sit far above or far below it.

Below is the 2026 allowance for 44 destinations, taken from each country’s own customs authority: cigarettes, cigars, loose and heated tobacco, and the minimum age. After the table: which categories stack, where one carton is already too many, and what happens when you go over.

🚬
One carton = 10 packs of 20 = 200 cigarettes. Several customs authorities count in packs rather than sticks — Mexico, Peru and the Dominican Republic allow 20 packs, Brazil 10 — so check which unit the limit uses before you buy.

Cigarettes, cigars and loose tobacco: do the limits stack?

Most countries give you one tobacco bucket: 200 cigarettes or 50 cigars or 250 g, or a proportional mix. A few let you carry each category on top of the others.

United States stacks: 200 cigarettes and 100 cigars per person.
Chile stacks: 400 cigarettes, 50 cigars and 500 g of pipe tobacco.
South Africa stacks: 200 cigarettes, 20 cigars and 250 g of cigarette or pipe tobacco.
Turkey lists every category: 600 cigarettes, 100 cigarillos, 50 cigars, 250 g of cut tobacco and 250 g of pipe tobacco.
UK picks one: 200 cigarettes or 100 cigarillos or 50 cigars or 250 g — or a proportional mix, never all of them.
Georgia mixes within one total, counted per 30 days: 200 cigarettes, 50 cigars, 50 cigarillos or 250 g, or a combination inside the total.

Heated tobacco, nicotine pouches and e-liquid

Heated-tobacco sticks and nicotine products are where customs pages go quiet. Of the countries we cover, only a few name them:

Japan: 10 packs of heat-not-burn tobacco, counted as its own limit alongside 200 cigarettes, 50 cigars or 250 g.
Switzerland: 250 cigarettes, cigars or heated tobacco products per person per day.
Bahrain: 400 cigarettes or heated tobacco sticks, or 400 nicotine pouches.
South Korea: nicotine e-liquid up to 20 ml sits inside the tobacco allowance.
Fiji: chewing tobacco is prohibited outright.

Vape devices follow separate rules in most countries — see Can You Bring a Vape on a Plane? Country-by-Country Rules.

Where you can bring more than a carton

Turkey: 600 cigarettes on the Foreign Ministry duty-free list — the highest limit in this guide.
Chile, Bahrain, Qatar: 400 cigarettes. Qatar also caps the tobacco at QAR 3,000 in value, with duty charged on the excess.
Kuwait and Oman: up to 400 cigarettes, but not for frequent travellers or anyone crossing the border regularly.
China: 400 cigarettes is the red-channel line — carry 400 or more, or 500 g or more of tobacco, and you must declare.
🔞
Minimum ages differ. 17 in the UK, New Zealand and Switzerland; 18 in most countries; 19 in South Korea; 20 in Taiwan. Under the limit age you get no tobacco allowance at all.

What happens if you go over the tobacco limit

Going over rarely makes the cigarettes illegal. It means duty, a held bag, or — in a few countries — a fine. The details are specific:

Australia: duty on the whole category you exceeded.
Singapore: everything is taxed, and unpaid duty is an offence.
Thailand: bringing more than the limit is treated strictly and can bring heavy fines.
Maldives: anything above the limit is held by Customs for up to 30 days — collect it at the departure terminal.
UAE: customs duty is charged on the excess.
Egypt: the quantity you buy as a tourist is recorded in your passport.
🧾
Limits are not always per trip. Georgia counts per 30 days and Switzerland per person per day. Mexico’s separate US$500 goods allowance cannot be used for tobacco.

Before you fly — checklist

1
Check the tobacco allowance for the country you are landing in, not the one you are leaving.
2
Check the unit: sticks, packs, or grams.
3
Check whether cigarettes, cigars and loose tobacco stack or share one total.
4
Check your age against the minimum age.
5
Going over? Declare it and pay — undeclared tobacco is seized.
