Personal Item Size by Airline 2026: Official Limits in Inches and cm

Carry-On Size·Updated October 2026·6 min read

A personal item is the free small item that goes under the seat in front of you. It is not always a backpack: the airlines’ own examples are a purse, handbag, laptop bag, briefcase or small backpack. The limit is a number at eight of the airlines whose own pages we read, from 17 × 10 × 9 inches at United to 18 × 14 × 8 inches at American and Frontier, and none at Delta, Alaska, Southwest and Hawaiian. 🎒 What counts as a personal item

Airlines describe it by example, and the examples differ. A backpack is only one of them.

AirlineExamples in the airline's own words
AmericanA purse or small handbag
DeltaA purse, laptop bag or item of similar size
JetBlueA purse, small backpack, briefcase, laptop and similar
SouthwestA purse (including crossbody bags), briefcase, laptop case, backpack, pillow, blanket or small camera
FrontierPurses, totes, computer bags, briefcases and kids' backpacks. Large backpacks are listed as carry-on examples
UnitedPurses, backpacks and laptop bags
HawaiianA purse, briefcase or laptop bag
💡
Read your airline’s list. American’s own example is a purse or small handbag, and Frontier lists large backpacks as carry-on bags, not personal items. JetBlue, Southwest and United do name a backpack.

What does not count

American: diaper bags (one per child), a breast pump, a small soft-sided cooler of breast milk, child safety seats, strollers and medical or mobility devices do not count as your personal item or carry-on.
Southwest: neck pillows do not count toward the carry-on limit.
Frontier: medical devices, wheelchairs and essential baby gear can be carried or checked at no charge.

❓ Airlines that publish no number

These four airlines say only that the item must fit under the seat in front of you. Charts that print a size for them are not quoting the airline’s own pages.

💡
No number does not mean no limit. The test is the space under the seat, and a crew member or gate agent decides. A soft bag that squashes flat is safer than a stiff one.

🎒 When the personal item is the only bag you get

United: on most Basic Economy trips you can bring one personal item and no carry-on.
Air Canada and WestJet: Economy Basic (Air Canada) and UltraBasic (WestJet) fares bring only a personal item on many routes.
Frontier: the personal item is the only free bag. The carry-on is a paid add-on.
Ryanair: every fare includes only the small bag; a bigger cabin bag comes with the paid Priority add-on.

🧮 Flying more than one airline?

Size to the tightest limit you will meet. Among the published sizes, the thinnest side is 6 inches at WestJet and Air Canada, and 8 inches at American, JetBlue, Breeze and Frontier. United’s bag is the narrowest at 10 inches on its second side. A soft backpack that packs down to about 6 inches deep is the safest choice across all of them.

Before you fly

1
Measure the packed bag, with straps and outside pockets, not the empty one.
2
Check your fare, not just the airline: Basic fares can leave you with the personal item only.
3
No size published? Pack so the bag sits flat under the seat in front without being pushed in.
4
Keep documents, medicine and chargers in the personal item, so they stay with you if the carry-on is gate-checked.

❓ Quick answers

What counts as a personal item?

A small item that fits under the seat in front of you. The airlines' own examples are a purse, handbag, laptop bag, briefcase, small backpack, small camera, and on Southwest also a blanket.

Is a backpack a personal item?

Often, if it fits under the seat. JetBlue, Southwest and United name a backpack as an example. American's example is a purse or small handbag, and Frontier lists kids' backpacks and calls large backpacks carry-on bags.

What size is a personal item on a plane?

It depends on the airline. American and Frontier allow 18 × 14 × 8 inches, JetBlue and Breeze 17 × 13 × 8, United 17 × 10 × 9, WestJet 16 × 6 × 13, Air Canada 13 × 17 × 6 and Ryanair 40 × 30 × 20 cm.

Does Frontier check personal item size?

Yes. Frontier says personal item size is checked at boarding, and an item over 18 × 14 × 8 inches is charged, and the Bag Options page sets a 35 lb limit.

What is the Delta personal item size?

Delta publishes no size. It says the personal item must fit under the seat in front of you.

Is the personal item size the same on Alaska and Southwest?

Neither airline publishes a number. Both say only that the item must fit under the seat.

What size backpack can I use as a personal item?

One that fits the tightest limit you will meet. A soft backpack within 17 × 13 × 8 inches fits JetBlue, Breeze and American; United is narrower at 17 × 10 × 9, and WestJet and Air Canada are only 6 inches deep.

Do I get a personal item on Basic Economy?

Yes, on every airline listed here the personal item is included. On several Basic fares it is the only bag you may bring.
