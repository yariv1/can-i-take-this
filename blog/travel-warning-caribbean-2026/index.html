🌀 Hurricane season: the warning that applies to every island

No government level reflects the weather, so check it separately. NOAA’s National Hurricane Center says the Atlantic hurricane season, which includes the Caribbean Sea, runs from June 1 to November 30, with the peak around September 10 and most activity from mid-August to mid-October.

🧭 How to read a Caribbean travel warning

🔍
Compare all three columns. The US, UK and Canada can rate the same island differently, because each weighs crime, health and politics in its own way.
🗺️
Check for regional advisories. Canada marks them with “+ regional advisories” and the UK marks “(parts)”: a rating for an island can hide a much higher risk in one area.
📅
Read the date. A fresh date after a news event is a sign to read the full advice again.
🛡️
Think about insurance. The UK warns that your travel insurance could be invalidated if you travel against FCDO advice, and only your insurer can say whether a claim is valid.

❓ Quick answers

Is there a Caribbean travel advisory or travel warning?

There is no single Caribbean travel advisory for the whole region. The US, UK and Canada each rate every island separately, so the level depends on the island and the government. The table above shows all of them and refreshes every 6 hours.

Which Caribbean islands have a travel warning?

The section “Caribbean islands with a travel warning right now” above lists every island at US Level 3 or 4, Canada Level 3 or 4, or under a UK warning against travel. It is rebuilt from the official feeds, so it stays current.

Is it safe to travel to the Caribbean right now?

It depends on the island. Most islands are at normal precautions with all three governments, a few are at increased caution because of crime, and a small number carry a serious warning. Check the island you plan to visit in the table, then read the government’s own page.

What does a Level 2 travel advisory mean in the Caribbean?

The US says Level 2 is “exercise increased caution”. It is used for many popular destinations and usually points to crime or weather, not a ban.

What do Level 3 and Level 4 mean?

In the US system Level 3 is “reconsider travel” and Level 4 is “do not travel”. Canada’s equivalents are “avoid non-essential travel” and “avoid all travel”. The UK uses “advise against all but essential travel” and “advise against all travel”.

When is Caribbean hurricane season?

NOAA says the Atlantic hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30, with the peak around September 10.

Does a travel warning stop me from traveling?

No. These are advice, not bans. They can still affect your travel insurance, so check your policy.

Where does this page get its levels?

From the US State Department feed, the UK government’s content API for FCDO travel advice and Canada’s travel advice data. Nothing is entered by hand.
