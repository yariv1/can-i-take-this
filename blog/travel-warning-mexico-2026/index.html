Travel Warning Mexico (2026): State-by-State US, UK and Canada Advisory Levels

Travel Safety·Levels checked 8 Oct 2026, 12:10 UTC·8 min read

Mexico does not have one travel warning: the US rates the whole country at Level 2 (“exercise increased caution”) but rates some states at Level 3 and Level 4, and the UK and Canada single out other states and areas. Which warning applies to you depends on the state you will be in, so the table below gives the US, UK and Canadian level for every Mexican state, with the reason, and the section after it answers the question for popular destinations such as Cancún, Los Cabos and Mexico City. 🛑 The real reason behind the Level 4 warnings

The US puts the highest warning on states where cartels and criminal groups fight for control, and where the US government cannot help much. This is what the State Department says for each Level 4 state:

🚫
Colima: Do not travel due to terrorism, crime, and kidnapping.
🚫
Guerrero: Do not travel due to terrorism, crime and kidnapping. U.S. government employees may not travel to any area in the state of Guerrero, including tourist areas Acapulco, Zihuatanejo, Taxco, and Ixtapa.
🚫
Michoacán: Do not travel due to terrorism and crime.
🚫
Sinaloa: Do not travel due to terrorism and crime. U.S. government employees may travel to Mazatlan by air or sea only: travel is limited to the historic town center from Avenida Gutierrez Najera to the ocean. U.S. government employees may not travel to other areas in the state of Sinaloa.
🚫
Tamaulipas: Do not travel due to terrorism, crime, and kidnapping.
🚫
Zacatecas: Do not travel due to terrorism and crime.

Level 3 states (Reconsider travel) carry the same reasons with fewer restrictions. Read the full state text on the State Department page before you plan a road trip: it lists exactly which highways and cities US government employees may use.

🚗 The rules the US tells its own staff, and advises citizens, to follow in Mexico

Due to security risks, U.S. citizens should follow the same restrictions as U.S. government employees while traveling. The restrictions for US government employees are:

🌙
May not travel between cities after dark.
🚕
Must rely on dispatched vehicles from regulated taxi stands or app-based services like Uber or Cabify and may not wave down taxis on the street.
👥
Should avoid traveling alone, especially in remote areas.
🛣️
May not drive between Mexican border cities and the interior of Mexico, with limited exceptions.
🚧
At a road checkpoint, comply. The State Department says fleeing or ignoring instructions can lead to you being hurt or killed.
📍
Enroll in STEP. The State Department asks US travelers to enroll in the Smart Traveler Enrollment Program for alerts from the US embassy.
🛡️
Buy travel insurance. The State Department highly recommends it, with evacuation, medical and trip cancellation cover. The UK warns that insurance could be invalidated if you travel against FCDO advice.

🧭 How to read a Mexico travel warning

🔍
Read the state, not the country. A Level 2 country can contain a Level 4 state, and a Level 4 state can contain a city the UK or Canada treats as an exception.
✈️
Check how you arrive. Several exceptions only apply if you travel by air or sea, for example Mazatlán and Manzanillo. Driving there is not covered by the exception.
🗺️
Compare all three columns. The US, UK and Canada weigh crime differently, so the same state can be at different levels.
📅
Check the dates. A fresh date after a news event is a sign to read the full advice again.

❓ Quick answers

Is there a travel warning for Mexico?

Yes, but not one for the whole country. The US rates Mexico as a whole at Level 2, “exercise increased caution”, and rates individual states from Level 1 to Level 4. The UK and Canada warn against travel to specific states and areas. The table above shows every state.

Which Mexican states have a Level 4 travel warning?

At our last check (Oct 6, 2026) the US listed six states at Level 4, Do not travel: Colima, Guerrero, Michoacán, Sinaloa, Tamaulipas, Zacatecas. The table above is the current reference.

Is it safe to travel to Mexico right now?

It depends on the state and on where you go inside it. The US says many violent crimes take place in Mexico, and also that conditions vary widely from state to state. Check the state in the table and the destination cards, then read the government’s own advice.

Is Cancún under a travel warning?

Cancún is in Quintana Roo, which the US rates Level 2. The UK and Canada do not list Quintana Roo in their regional warnings. The US still warns about crime and says to stay in well-lit pedestrian streets and tourist zones after dark.

Is Mexico City safe to visit?

The US rates Mexico City Level 2 and says to exercise extra caution, especially at night, outside popular tourist areas. The UK and Canada do not list it in their regional warnings.

What does Level 2 mean for Mexico?

In the US system Level 2 is “exercise increased caution”. For Mexico it is the national level, and some states carry Level 3 or 4 on top of it.

Does a travel warning stop me from traveling to Mexico?

No. These are advice, not a legal ban. They can still matter for your travel insurance, so check your policy.

Where does this page get its levels?

The national levels and the UK and Canada regions come from the official US, UK and Canadian sources and refresh every 6 hours. The US state levels are read from the State Department page at each update of this article, and the page warns you if the US advisory changes in between.
