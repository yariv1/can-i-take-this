Delayed Flight Compensation 2026: What US Airlines Owe You

Airline Fees·Updated October 2026·7 min read

US law does not make an airline pay cash for a delayed flight. What you get is a refund if you decide not to fly, plus the promises each airline puts in its own customer service plan: rebooking, a meal and a hotel when the delay is the airline’s fault. Of the five airlines we checked, only Southwest promises a voucher worth at least $75 and only JetBlue promises points (5,000 for TrueBlue members). Europe and the UK are different: they pay a fixed amount in cash.

Check A mechanical problem counts as the airline’s fault. Weather and air traffic control do not: American says you pay for your own meals and hotel, and Southwest promises only rebooking.

Refund DOT says you are entitled to a refund if the airline cancels, or significantly delays, a flight and you decline the alternative. It must arrive within 7 business days for a card, 20 calendar days for other payments.

File Southwest: the LUV voucher form within one year. Delta: the reimbursement request for meals, hotel and transport. JetBlue: nothing for the points; they arrive automatically.

What US law gives you

⚖️
The government does not require cash for a delay. On 17 November 2025 DOT withdrew its December 2024 advance notice that asked whether airlines should be required to pay cash compensation and provide free rebooking and meals for significant delays. It never became a rule.

The rule that does exist is about refunds. DOT says a “significant delay” is an arrival 3 hours or more later (domestic) or 6 hours or more later (international) than scheduled, and also covers an early departure by the same amount, a change of airport, added connections or a downgrade. If you choose not to travel, the airline owes a refund automatically: within 7 business days if you paid by card and within 20 calendar days for other payments. A separate DOT rule published on 3 September 2026 takes effect on 19 October 2026. It changes how airlines report the causes of delays, putting ten events Congress listed in their own reporting category. It does not add any payment to what you can claim.

Flying to or from Europe? A different rulebook

Delay rights in Europe are a fixed cash amount, set by distance and by how late you arrive. For a transatlantic flight, the distance is over 3,500 km.

Which flights are covered matters for Americans. EU rules apply to every flight leaving an EU airport for a country outside the EU, on any airline, and to flights arriving in the EU only when an EU airline operates them. A Delta flight from Paris to New York is covered; a Delta flight from New York to Paris is not. UK rules follow a similar logic: a flight from a UK airport to the U.S. is covered on any airline, including a US airline, but a flight arriving in the UK from the U.S. is covered only on a UK or EU airline. The EU also says airlines do not owe compensation for delays caused by extraordinary circumstances such as adverse weather, air traffic management decisions or security risks, and that the airline must prove it. If you have already received benefits for the same journey under non-EU law, the EU rules do not add a second payment. Unlike the US, UK and EU airlines must also give you meals and, for overnight delays, a hotel, once the wait reaches 2, 3 or 4 hours depending on the distance.

❓ Quick answers

Do airlines have to pay you for a delayed flight in the US?

No. DOT does not require cash compensation for a delay, and on 17 November 2025 it withdrew its 2024 proposal to require it. You are entitled to a refund if you decline a significantly delayed flight, and each airline promises its own meals and hotel when the delay is its fault.

How long does a flight have to be delayed to get compensation?

For meals, the airlines we checked start at 3 hours. For a refund, DOT counts a significant delay as 3 hours or more for a domestic itinerary and 6 hours or more for an international one. In Europe, cash compensation starts at an arrival 3 hours or more late.

Does American Airlines pay compensation for a delayed flight?

Its customer service plan promises rebooking, a food and beverage credit at 3 hours or more, and a hotel voucher with transport for an overnight delay, when American caused the delay. It lists no cash payment.

Does Delta pay compensation for a delayed flight?

Delta lets you request reimbursement of reasonable meal costs for a delay or cancellation of 3 hours or more caused by Delta, and a hotel and ground transport if you are stranded overnight. It does not reimburse weather or air traffic control delays.

Does United compensate for delays?

United promises a meal voucher after a wait of more than 3 hours and hotel and ride vouchers for an overnight stay, when the cause is within its control. Its plan lists no cash payment for the delay itself.

Does Southwest give a voucher for a delay?

Yes, for delays and cancellations within its control: a transferable voucher of at least $75 if you arrive 3 hours or more late, requested at Southwest.com/DelayForm within one year. Only one voucher per disruption.

What does JetBlue give for a delay?

For a controllable delay of more than 3 hours JetBlue gives TrueBlue members 5,000 points, plus a $12 meal voucher to every ticketed customer when the arrival delay is 3 hours or more.

Do I get compensation if the delay is caused by weather?

Not from the airline in the US: American says you pay for your own meals and hotel, Southwest only rebooks you, and JetBlue does not compensate for uncontrollable events. In the EU and UK, adverse weather is an extraordinary circumstance, and the airline does not owe the cash payment if it proves the link.

Does EU compensation apply to my US airline flight?

Only if the flight leaves an EU airport, on any airline, or an EU airline operates it into the EU. A US airline flight arriving in the EU from the US is not covered, and a US airline flight arriving in the UK from the US is not covered by UK rules either.
