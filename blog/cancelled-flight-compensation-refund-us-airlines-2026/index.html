Cancelled Flight Compensation 2026: Refund Rights and What US Airlines Owe You

Airline Fees·Updated October 2026·8 min read

If an airline cancels your flight, US law says you can have your money back, whatever the reason for the cancellation, if you do not want the new flight or the credit it offers. There is no cash “extra” on top of the refund in the US: the Department of Transportation requires the refund, not compensation. The rule is easy to lose in the airline’s wording, which pushes vouchers and credits first.

💵 Cash or voucher: what you are allowed to ask for

DOT’s Refunds page says that if an airline cancels a flight, it owes you a refund if you choose not to travel or to accept credits or vouchers. That is true for a cancellation caused by weather as much as one caused by the airline. The airline decides the fault only for the extras, such as a hotel.

If you take a credit, the airline must tell you the restrictions, the credit must stay valid for at least 5 years, and you must be told you can ask for a refund instead. You can still say no after reading the offer.

⏱️ The automatic refund and how fast it must come

If the airline offers you a new flight and you reject it, or if you do not respond, DOT says the refund is automatic to the original form of payment. These are the deadlines and the steps, in the order they happen:

1
Did the airline tell you about the cancellation?
Check Open the airline app or the email. Note the time of the notice and the new offer. The airline must tell you that you have the right to a refund.
2
Do you want the new flight?
Your choice If yes, accept it. You then have no refund. If no, say so in the app or on the phone, or simply do not answer: a declined or unanswered offer turns into a refund.
3
Did the airline offer a voucher or credit instead?
Ask You can take it, but it must be valid for at least 5 years and the airline has to tell you the restrictions and that you may take a refund instead. Say “refund to my original payment” if you want the money.
4
When must the money arrive?
7 or 20 days DOT: within 7 business days if you paid by credit card, within 20 calendar days for other payments. (One paragraph of the DOT page says “20 business days”; we use the 20 calendar days it gives elsewhere.)
5
What if it does not arrive?
Escalate Ask the airline in writing, then dispute the charge with your card company, then file a complaint with DOT. Keep your confirmation, the cancellation notice and every receipt.

🔄 What counts as cancelled or significantly changed

A cancellation is the easy case. A schedule change can also give you a refund right if it is big enough. DOT defines “significant” as follows:

A change counts as “significant” whenDomesticInternational
The flight leaves earlier or lands later than scheduled by3 hours or more6 hours or more
The airline changes your origin or destination airportYesYes
The airline adds a connectionYesYes
The airline moves you to a lower class (involuntary downgrade)Refund of the fare difference if you keep flyingRefund of the fare difference if you keep flying

If you choose to fly on the changed flight anyway, there is no refund for the change (your airline’s plan may still pay for a meal or a hotel). A non-refundable ticket on a flight that operates as scheduled is also not refundable.

🧾 Fees you can get back too

The ticket is not the only money. DOT lists the extras that come back when the service was not provided:

📌
Bought through a travel agent? If the agent is the merchant of record, it owes you the ticket refund, but the airline still owes the ancillary and bag refunds. Ask the agent first for the ticket and the airline for the add-ons.

Flying from Europe or the UK? Cash on top of the refund

The EU and UK rules go further than the US: for a flight cancelled less than 14 days before departure you can be owed 250, 400 or 600 euros (UK: 220, 350 or 520 pounds) on top of the refund or re-routing, unless the airline proves extraordinary circumstances. They follow the flight, not the airline. All the details, and who is covered, are in our EU261 article.

Before you fly

Book by credit card when you can. A card refund must reach you in 7 business days and you have a chargeback if it does not.
When the airline cancels, screenshot the notice and the offer before you tap anything.
Decide before you accept a credit: is it cash you want? Say “refund to original payment” in writing.
Claim the add-ons too: seats, Wi-Fi, bags. They are separate fees.
Booked through an agent? Contact the agent for the ticket, the airline for the extras.

❓ Quick answers

Do I get compensation for a cancelled flight in the US?

US law requires a refund, not extra cash. If the airline cancels and you decline the new flight or credit, DOT says you are owed your money back. Any hotel or meal comes from the airline’s own plan, not from a rule. Cash compensation on top of the refund exists in the EU and the UK.

Can I get a refund if the airline cancels my flight because of weather?

Yes. DOT says the refund applies when the airline cancels a flight regardless of the reason. Weather only matters for extras such as a hotel, which airlines generally do not pay for.

Can the airline give me a voucher instead of a refund?

It can offer one, but it has to tell you that you may take a refund instead, and the voucher must be valid for at least 5 years with its restrictions disclosed.

How long does a cancelled flight refund take?

DOT says within 7 business days if you paid by credit card, and within 20 calendar days for other forms of payment.

I already took the voucher. Can I still get a refund?

We did not find a DOT rule that lets you change your mind later, so ask the airline in writing and check the voucher’s own terms. Better: decide before you accept it.

Do I also get my bag fee and seat fee back?

Seat, Wi-Fi and similar fees come back if the service was not provided. A checked bag fee comes back when the bag is lost or significantly delayed and you filed a report. Delta’s contract refunds unused ancillary fees, including checked-bag fees, with the ticket when it cancels.

I booked through Expedia or another agent. Who refunds me?

DOT says an agent that is the merchant of record owes the ticket refund, while the airline owes the refunds of its ancillary and bag fees.

Can I cancel for free within 24 hours instead?

Only for tickets bought at least 7 days before departure, and only from the airline. It is a separate rule from the cancellation refund.

Airline contracts and government rules change; the airline’s own contract and the government pages below on the day you travel are the ones that count.
