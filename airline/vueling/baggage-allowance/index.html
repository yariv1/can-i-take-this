❓ Quick answers

What is the Vueling cabin bag size?

The free underseat bag is up to 40 × 30 × 20 cm. An overhead piece, if your fare includes it, is up to 55 × 40 × 20 cm and 10 kg.

Does Vueling Fly Light include a bag?

Only the underseat bag (40 × 30 × 20 cm). The overhead bag and a checked bag cost extra.

How much is a checked bag on Vueling?

Online, from 10 to 95 EUR for 15 kg, 18 to 99 EUR for 25 kg and 25 to 120 EUR for 30 kg, depending on the route; at the airport a 25 kg bag is from 50 to 160 EUR.

What happens if my bag is too big at the Vueling gate?

An underseat bag over the limit is checked for 60 to 75 EUR, and a piece over 55 × 40 × 20 cm for 110 to 140 EUR, per item and flight.

Checked 7 October 2026 on the airline's own pages. Baggage rules change by route, fare and date: the allowance on your ticket is the one that applies.
