Rebooking help, no delay cash
Turkish Airlines' US Customer Service Plan
✈ Turkish AirlinesCOMPENSATION

The US plan promises rebooking help and DOT bumping rules; it lists no cash for delays.

  • For delays, cancellations or misconnections it says it will make reasonable efforts to help you rebook your travel to your destination.
  • If you cannot reach your final destination on the original arrival date because of something under its control and rebooking is not possible, it may, at its direction, offer a free overnight hotel. If the cause is severe weather or other issues outside its control, it says in most cases there is no hotel, but it will try to give you the information to arrange your own.
  • If you are bumped (oversale) on a flight leaving the US, you are in most cases entitled to transport on another flight and/or compensation as DOT regulations require. It says it asks for volunteers first and gives each denied passenger a written statement of their rights. The DOT amounts are in our overbooked guide.
  • Refund: you can refund a ticket to or from the US within 24 hours of purchase for a full refund if it was bought at least 7 days before departure through its US sales channels.
  • The plan does not list a cash amount for a delay. See the cards for Turkish rules, Canada and EU departures below.

Care from a 2 hour delay
Delay under Turkish Airlines' own notice
✈ Turkish AirlinesCOMPENSATION

Its notice gives services and ticket rights by delay time; it lists no cash for a delay.

  • The notice says that for a denied boarding, cancellation or delay of at least two hours you can benefit from the rights under Turkey's Regulation on the Rights of Passengers Traveling by Air (SHGM). You must have a confirmed reservation and be at check-in at least 60 minutes before an international departure (45 minutes domestic).
  • Its service table lists information, free phone calls (twice), drinks, a snack or a main course depending on the time of day, transport fees for alternative vehicles, and accommodation with transport between the airport and the hotel.
  • Accommodation: if the delay is 8 hours or more, or an overnight stay becomes necessary regardless of the delay, accommodation and transport are provided, depending on the station's facilities.
  • Its table columns run from 16-59 minutes up to 300 minutes and longer and show ticket rights (free rebooking, or a refund). We could not read which service sits in which column, so check at the counter.
  • The Flight Disruptions page adds that for a delay you can change your flight or cancel and get a refund within rules set by the delay time.

Compensation unless it told you early
Cancellation under Turkish Airlines' own notice
✈ Turkish AirlinesCOMPENSATION

Cancel for a non-extraordinary reason and it assesses compensation, in Turkish Airlines products and services as well.

  • The notice says compensation, which includes Turkish Airlines products and services, is assessed when a flight is cancelled for reasons other than extraordinary circumstances, unless you were told at least two weeks before; or told two weeks to seven days before and offered a re-routing that leaves no more than 2 hours earlier and arrives no more than 4 hours later; or told less than seven days before and offered one that leaves no more than 1 hour earlier and arrives no more than 2 hours later.
  • It says offers can differ if the alternative flight arrives within 2 hours (1,500 km or less), 3 hours (1,500 to 3,500 km) or 4 hours (over 3,500 km) of the planned arrival.
  • The notice does not state a euro, dollar or lira amount. It says the Turkish lira equivalent is worked out at the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye's selling rate on the date of payment.
  • Tickets: you can change to another flight, or request a full refund, under certain rules (Flight Disruptions page). The notice describes a one-time free change within the ticket validity period, between 7 days before and 360 days after the flight date.
  • For a ticket bought with miles it lists 3,000 miles (0 to 1,500 km), 5,000 miles (1,500 to 3,500 km) and 10,000 miles (over 3,500 km).

Compensation assessed
Overbooking
✈ Turkish AirlinesCOMPENSATION

Turkish rules assess compensation for an overbooked flight; the notice names no amount, and DOT or Canadian rules set one on those routes.

  • Its Turkish-rules notice says a passenger denied boarding because of overbooking is assessed compensation including products and services. It lists no amount. People refused for medical, safety, security or travel-document reasons are outside the scope.
  • Downgrade: you are compensated with Turkish Airlines products and services in addition to the price difference.
  • Leaving the US: DOT rules apply, volunteers are asked first and you get a written statement (US plan).
  • To or from Canada, the Canadian page lists CAD 900 (arrival delayed under 6 hours), CAD 1,800 (6 to 9 hours) and CAD 2,400 (9 hours or more), paid as soon as operationally feasible and within 48 hours, when the denial was in its control.

CAD 400, 700 or 1,000
Canadian rules on its page
✈ Turkish AirlinesCOMPENSATION

For a delay or cancellation within its control, with 3 hours or more of arrival delay.

  • If the cancellation or delay is within its control and you reach your destination 3 hours or more late, and you were not told 14 days or more before: CAD 400 for an arrival 3 to 6 hours late, CAD 700 for 6 to 9 hours, CAD 1,000 for 9 hours or more. It is limited to CAD 400 when you take a refund instead of alternate travel.
  • No compensation if the cause is outside its control (it names weather or natural disaster, political instability, strikes, ground problems), if the cancellation or delay results from safety requirements, if you were told 14 days or more before, or if you already got compensation under another regime for the same event.
  • Claim within one year of the day your flight was delayed or cancelled.
  • The page applies to flights operated by Turkish Airlines and AnadoluJet; for a codeshare, contact the airline that operated it.
  • We did not read the page's care entitlements (food, hotel) for Canada.

EU law: 250, 400 or 600 euros
EU rules, not read on Turkish Airlines' pages
✈ Turkish AirlinesCOMPENSATION

EU rules apply to flights leaving the EU; we did not find a Turkish Airlines page for them.

  • Under the EU rules (Your Europe), for a flight leaving an EU airport and an arrival 3 hours or more late for a cause within the airline's control: 250 euros up to 1,500 km, 400 euros for 1,500 to 3,500 km, 600 euros for longer flights. Care and the refund or re-routing choice do not depend on the cause.
  • We did not read a Turkish Airlines notice for EU departures, so we cannot say how it handles claims. Our EU261 guide explains the steps.

No compensation
Extraordinary circumstances
✈ Turkish AirlinesCOMPENSATION

Turkish rules assess no compensation for these; change and refund rights still apply.

  • The notice says no compensation is assessed for irregularities caused by extraordinary situations: meteorological conditions, natural disasters, security risks, unexpected flight-safety deficiencies, strike, political instability.
  • The Flight Disruptions page says that for extraordinary circumstances you can change to a different flight under certain rules (a fare difference may apply) or get a refund under certain fare rules, and that the entire ticket fee may not be refunded for a cancellation under an extraordinary circumstance.
  • It lists pandemics, epidemics or diseases deemed an emergency, and weather or natural disasters that could endanger passengers, as examples.

Feedback form or call
Claiming from Turkish Airlines
✈ Turkish AirlinesCOMPENSATION

The notice points to the feedback form on its website and app, and to its call and WhatsApp number.

  • The notice says to submit your feedback through the feedback form on turkishairlines.com or its mobile app, or through its listed channels. You can also fill in the form in the in-flight entertainment system during the flight.
  • It lists telephone/WhatsApp +90 850 333 0 849, an address (Türk Hava Yolları Genel Yönetim Binası, Atatürk Havalimanı, Yeşilköy, 34149 İstanbul) and social media (@TK_HelpDesk).
  • From the US, its US plan names the 24/7 call centre (800) 874-8875 for refund problems. The Get in Touch page says it follows the compensation claim procedures required by local and international rules.
  • For Canada, claim within one year. We did not find a separate online compensation claim form on the pages we read.
