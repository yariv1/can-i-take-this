Emirates baggage allowance (2026): cabin & checked

Check

Emirates economy: 1 cabin bag up to 7 kg per passenger (55 × 38 × 22 cm). No separate personal item in Economy.

Cabin and checked baggage by fare class. Fees are shown as ranges — always confirm the exact figure with Emirates.

Source: Emirates official baggage policy

Economy (Special/Saver/Flex/Flex Plus)

Cabin: 1 cabin bag up to 7 kg per passenger (55 × 38 × 22 cm). No separate personal item in Economy.

Checked: Weight-concept routes: Special 20 kg · Saver 25 kg · Flex 30 kg · Flex Plus 35 kg per passenger. Piece-concept routes (Americas/Africa): Special 1 × 23 kg, higher fares 2 × 23 kg. Max 32 kg per bag.

Premium Economy

Cabin: 1 cabin bag up to 10 kg per passenger (55 × 38 × 22 cm).

Checked: Weight routes: ~35 kg. Piece routes: 2 × 23 kg per passenger.

Business

Cabin: 2 cabin bags per passenger (1 cabin bag + 1 briefcase/garment bag), up to 7 kg each.

Checked: 40 kg (weight routes) or 2 × 32 kg (piece routes) per passenger.

First

Cabin: 2 cabin bags per passenger, up to 7 kg each.

Checked: 50 kg (weight routes) or 2 × 32 kg (piece routes) per passenger.
