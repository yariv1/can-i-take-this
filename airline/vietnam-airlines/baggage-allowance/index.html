❓ Quick answers

What is the Vietnam Airlines carry-on size?

One piece up to 56 × 36 × 23 cm (22 × 14 × 9 in) and 10 kg, plus one accessory up to 40 × 30 × 15 cm.

How many checked bags are free on Vietnam Airlines in Economy?

1 piece of 23 kg on most routes, including Vietnam domestic, Europe, Australia and Hong Kong, China, Taiwan and South Korea. Routes to and from the Americas give 2 pieces of 23 kg. Japan depends on route and aircraft.

What is the Vietnam Airlines checked bag size limit?

158 cm (62 in) in length + width + height per piece.

Is the carry-on weight different on long routes?

Yes. To and from the US, Australia and Europe, Economy hand baggage totals 12 kg (one piece up to 10 kg plus an accessory); on other routes Economy is 10 kg in total.

Checked 7 October 2026 on the airline's own pages. Baggage rules change by route, fare and date: the allowance on your ticket is the one that applies.
