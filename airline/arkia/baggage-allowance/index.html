🧳 Checked baggage

  • International: you can order a checked bag for a fee when you buy the ticket and until 48 hours before check-in opens. Over the included weight, an excess weight fee applies.
  • Domestic: you can order a checked bag when you book, during web check-in, or at the check-in counter (the most expensive option). No bag may weigh more than 20 kg, and Arkia may refuse a heavier one.
  • Take your checked bag to the Arkia check-in counter to have it tagged.

❓ Quick answers

What is the Arkia hand bag size?

Up to 40 × 30 × 20 cm (16 × 12 × 8 in), stored under the seat in front of you. It is included in the ticket price.

Is a trolley included on Arkia?

Only if your reservation says so, on a domestic ticket at the maximum regulated price, or on a U.S. ticket ordered from 21 February 2025. The trolley limit is 8 kg and 56 × 45 × 25 cm.

Is a checked bag included on Arkia?

On most international flights not unless your reservation says so; flights to the U.S. include one bag of 23 kg.

What is the Arkia domestic bag weight limit?

20 kg (44 lb) per bag; Arkia can refuse a heavier one.
