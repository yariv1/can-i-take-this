✈ American AirlinesWI-FI

American lists all single-aisle aircraft as having free Wi-Fi: every Airbus A319, A320 and A321 and every Boeing 737.

🔌 How to connect

  1. Switch your device to airplane mode and turn Wi-Fi on.
  2. Connect to the "aainflight.com" Wi-Fi signal.
  3. Open a browser and enter aainflight.com if you are not redirected.
  4. Select "Free Wi-Fi" and sign in with your AAdvantage login, or select "View Wi-Fi plans" for other options.

🎬 Entertainment

American says that on most flights you can stream its free library of movies, music and TV shows to your phone, tablet or laptop on Wi-Fi-equipped flights, using the American Airlines app. It does not list which aircraft have seatback screens, so we do not list that here.
