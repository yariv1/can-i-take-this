🧳 Checked bag fees and size on domestic flights

These are the fees Delta lists for Delta Comfort and Delta Main customers on flights within the U.S., without a Delta SkyMiles American Express card, Medallion status or an active-military exception.

  • Online check-in: you can select the number of bags you plan to check and pay, or redeem miles (domestically, at most airports), in the 24-hour check-in window on delta.com, in the Fly Delta app or at a kiosk at the airport.
  • Number of bags: up to 10 per passenger on flights operated by Delta or Delta Shuttle, and up to 4 on Delta Connection carrier flights. Delta can limit excess baggage if it prevents carrying up to two bags per passenger.

⚖️ Overweight and oversized bags

Delta charges a separate fee for each limit a bag exceeds (size, weight, quantity), so a bag that is extra, overweight and oversize pays three fees.

Situation (within the U.S., Canada, Puerto Rico, USVI)Fee (each way)
Overweight bag, 51 to 70 lb (23 to 31.75 kg)$100
Overweight bag, 71 to under 100 lb (32 to 45.36 kg)$200
Oversized bag, 63 to 80 in (161 to 203 cm)$200

  • Between North, Central or South America and Europe or North Africa: overweight 51 to 70 lb is $100 (or $120 CAD or €85 when departing Canada or Europe), and bags over 70 lb (31.75 kg) are not allowed.
  • Also limited to 70 lb (31.75 kg): flights to, from or within Australia and New Zealand, and flights to, from or through Europe, South Africa or the United Arab Emirates.
  • Extra bags (3 to 10) on international routes: $200 on most routes, $285 USD between North, Central or South America and Europe or North Africa. CAD or EUR amounts are charged when leaving Canada or Europe.

🏅 Who gets bags free

  • Medallion members: the first checked bag fee is waived with Delta Medallion status, or as a SkyTeam Elite or Elite Plus member. This also covers companions on the reservation, up to 8, on Delta-operated flights (not from Paris CDG or Amsterdam AMS).
  • Medallion, Delta Main and Comfort within the U.S. and Canada: Silver 1, Gold 2, Platinum 3 and Diamond 3 free bags, as listed in Delta's allowance table. Delta One and Delta First: up to 3 free bags at 70 lb (32 kg) each. Delta Premium Select: 3 free bags.
  • Delta SkyMiles American Express card: first checked bag free on Delta flights booked with the card, for the cardholder and up to 8 companions on the reservation. The Basic cardholder also gets a second bag free on domestic flights. The waiver does not apply to overweight or oversize bags.
  • Military: exceptions apply for active military; we did not read that page.

❓ Quick answers

What is the Delta carry-on size?

22 × 14 × 9 in (56 × 35 × 23 cm), including handles and wheels, and no more than 45 linear inches (114 cm) in total. You can also bring one personal item that fits under the seat.

Is a carry-on free on Delta?

Yes. Each passenger can bring 1 carry-on bag and 1 personal item free, with exceptions for infants-in-arms and pets in the cabin. On Delta Connection flights with 50 seats or less, only personal items are allowed on board.

How much is the first checked bag on Delta?

$45 each way on domestic Delta Main and Comfort flights for a bag up to 50 lb (23 kg), and $55 for the second, unless you have a Delta SkyMiles Amex card, Medallion status or a military exception. International fees depend on the route and are shown in Delta's Baggage Calculator.

What is the Delta checked bag weight limit?

50 lb (23 kg) for a standard checked bag. Heavier bags pay $100 for 51 to 70 lb and $200 for 71 to under 100 lb within the U.S. and Canada; bags of 100 lb or more are not permitted. Some international routes cap bags at 70 lb.

What is the Delta checked bag size limit?

62 inches (157 cm) in length + width + height. Bags of 63 to 80 inches pay $200 each way, and bags larger than 80 inches (203 cm) are not permitted.

How many bags can I check on Delta?

Up to 10 per passenger on Delta or Delta Shuttle flights and up to 4 on Delta Connection flights. Bags 3 to 10 cost $200 each on domestic flights.

Checked 7 October 2026 on the airline's own pages. Baggage rules change by route, fare and date: the allowance on your ticket is the one that applies.
