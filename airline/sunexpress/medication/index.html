Over 100 ml (3.4 oz)
✈ SunExpressMEDICATION

SunExpress allows medicines up to 2 litres per passenger, 500 ml each, and points to other liquid rules.

  • SunExpress: non-radioactive medicines, with toilet articles and cosmetics such as aerosols, perfumes and hair sprays, are allowed up to 2 litres per passenger, each tube at most 500 ml.
  • SunExpress: other liquid and customs legislation must also be considered.

International flights
✈ SunExpressMEDICATION

The country you fly to sets the rules for your medication, not SunExpress.

  • SunExpress does not list destination rules for medication. For Turkey see our medication rules for Turkey; for other countries check the destination's rules first, because controlled medicines (strong painkillers, ADHD or sleep medication, for example) are restricted in many places.
