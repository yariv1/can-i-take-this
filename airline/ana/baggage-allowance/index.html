❓ Quick answers

What is the ANA carry-on size on international flights?

Within 55 × 40 × 25 cm and no more than 115 cm in length + width + height, plus a personal item within 40 × 30 × 20 cm, 10 kg combined.

What is the ANA checked bag weight limit?

23 kg (50 lb) per piece, or 32 kg (70 lb) per piece in the higher cabins, and 158 cm in length + width + height.

How many checked bags are free on ANA?

It depends on cabin and fare: 3 pieces, 1 to 2 pieces or 0 to 2 pieces on the page's table. Your reservation shows the allowance for your ticket.

Can ANA refuse a bag that fits the size limit?

Yes. Depending on the route or aircraft type, a bag within 292 cm may not be accepted because of limited cargo space.

Checked 7 October 2026 on the airline's own pages. Baggage rules change by route, fare and date: the allowance on your ticket is the one that applies.
