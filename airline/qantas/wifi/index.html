✈ QantasWI-FI

Qantas lists all Airbus A321XLR, all Airbus A220-300, all Boeing 787 and all Airbus A330-200 aircraft as Wi-Fi connected.

  • Qantas says its Wi-Fi is free across all cabins on Wi-Fi-connected aircraft, with unlimited data that lets you stream.
  • Qantas says WiFi is available on QantasLink flights operated by A220-300 aircraft.
  • Qantas recommends checking the Qantas app 24 to 48 hours before you travel: add your flight to My Trips and select View onboard experience.

✈ QantasWI-FI

Qantas says flights across Australia and New Zealand get fast WiFi that supports browsing and streaming. On international flights outside Australia and New Zealand you currently get a varied connection.

  • A varied connection keeps you connected for the essentials and may not support video streaming. Qantas says coverage is currently not available over China, India, the South Pacific Ocean or the Indian Ocean.
  • Qantas says voice and video calls are not permitted, and data roaming is not permitted.

🔌 How to connect

  1. Enable Flight Mode.
  2. Select the "Qantas Free Wi-Fi" network in your device settings.
  3. Follow the prompts on the "You're connected" screen.
  4. If the page does not load, type wifi.qantas.com into your browser.
