Check

Go (base fare)

Cabin: 1 personal item only (45 × 35 × 20 cm) free. A carry-on bag is a paid add-on (~$65–100).

Checked: All checked bags paid (~$50–90); cheapest when added at booking. Max 18 kg before overweight fees.

Go Big / bundles

Cabin: 1 carry-on + 1 personal item included, per passenger.

Checked: Checked bags included on higher bundles.
