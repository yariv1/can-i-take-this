✈ easyJetWI-FI

AirFi says its system is installed on all 327 easyJet aircraft and creates a local WiFi network on board. Access to the content is free.

  • The portal gives games, journey-specific content, airline information and the in-flight shop menu on your own phone, tablet or laptop.
  • AirFi says you do not need to download an app before the flight or share personal information to connect.
