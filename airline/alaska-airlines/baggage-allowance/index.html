Check

Alaska Airlines economy: 1 carry-on (56 × 36 × 23 cm) + 1 personal item, per passenger (free on all fares incl. Saver).

Saver / Main

Cabin: 1 carry-on (56 × 36 × 23 cm) + 1 personal item, per passenger (free on all fares incl. Saver).

Checked: No free checked bag — first bag ~$35–45. Max 23 kg / 157 cm per bag.

First Class

Cabin: 1 carry-on + 1 personal item, per passenger.

Checked: 2 checked bags up to 32 kg each, per passenger.
