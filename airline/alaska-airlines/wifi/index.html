Free WiFi
Thanks to T-Mobile, on Starlink aircraft
✈ Alaska AirlinesWI-FI

The release says that on Starlink-equipped aircraft, inflight Wi-Fi is complimentary for Atmos Rewards members, thanks to T-Mobile.

  • Existing members connect automatically through a streamlined portal. Enrollment in Atmos Rewards is free and takes seconds, before or during travel.
  • With Starlink you can stream live sports, movies and shows, browse, use apps, play video games and send messages, gate to gate.

✈ Alaska AirlinesWI-FI

The release says that from June on select flights, and on all flights by mid-July, guests need Atmos Rewards to access the complimentary Starlink Wi-Fi.

  • Guests 16 and older can join Atmos Rewards online. For guests under 16 a parent or legal guardian completes enrollment by contacting Alaska Airlines.
  • The release does not say what a guest who does not join pays.

✈ Alaska AirlinesWI-FI

The release says Alaska expects to finish Starlink on the remaining mainline fleet by 2027, and to have its entire widebody fleet done this fall, including the Boeing 787-9.

  • Alaska and Hawaiian published an installation tracker. The release does not list individual aircraft types.
