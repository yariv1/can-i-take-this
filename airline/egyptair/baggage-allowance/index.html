EgyptAir baggage allowance (2026): cabin & checked

Check

EgyptAir economy: 1 cabin bag up to 8 kg (55 × 40 × 23 cm) plus 1 personal item. US flights: 1 carry-on and 1 personal item only.

Cabin and checked baggage by fare class. Fees are shown as ranges — always confirm the exact figure with EgyptAir.

Source: EgyptAir official baggage policy

Economy (International)

Cabin: 1 cabin bag up to 8 kg (55 × 40 × 23 cm) plus 1 personal item. US flights: 1 carry-on and 1 personal item only.

Checked: 2 checked bags included, up to 23 kg each. Domestic Economy: 1 × 23 kg. Asmara (Eritrea) tickets: 1 piece from Jan 2026. Max 32 kg / 158 cm (confirm on egyptair.com).

Business / First

Cabin: 2 cabin bags (8 kg each) plus 1 personal item.

Checked: 2 checked bags included, up to 32 kg / 158 cm each.
