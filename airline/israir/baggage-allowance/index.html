  • Only one personal item is allowed per passenger; extra bags and shopping bags are not accepted in the cabin.
  • Cabin space is limited to about 50 bags per flight. Once it is full, extra hand bags go to the hold and come out on the baggage belt at the destination, and paying for a trolley does not guarantee it stays in the cabin.
  • A bag over the size or weight limit is sent to the hold and charged as checked baggage.
  • Liquids in the cabin: containers of 100 ml (3.4 oz) or less, all in one resealable plastic bag, 1 litre in total, one bag per passenger. Medicines and special food such as baby food are allowed outside the bag with a suitable medical certificate.
  • Strollers of every type, including umbrella strollers, go to the hold and are handed over at the screening counters or before boarding.

❓ Quick answers

What is the Israir personal item size?

40 × 30 × 20 cm (16 × 12 × 8 in), under the seat in front of you. It is free on every ticket and you may take only one.

Is a trolley free on Israir?

On international flights no: a trolley up to 55 × 40 × 25 cm and 10 kg costs $30 each way when bought in advance. On domestic flights and on New York flights it is free or included.

How much is a checked bag on Israir?

On international flights the first bag up to 23 kg costs $65 each way when added online or in My Israir up to 6 hours before the flight, and more at the airport. New York flights include a 23 kg bag in Economy.

What is the Israir checked bag weight limit?

23 kg (50 lb) per piece. On the first bag you can add up to 30 kg on international flights for an extra fee, or up to 32 kg on New York and domestic flights.

Does Israir keep my trolley in the cabin if I pay for it?

Not always. Space is limited to about 50 hand bags per flight; when it is full, extra bags go to the hold and are collected on the baggage belt.

Checked 7 October 2026 on the airline's own pages. Baggage rules change by route, fare and date: the allowance on your ticket is the one that applies.
