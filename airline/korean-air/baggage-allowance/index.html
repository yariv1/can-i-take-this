Korean Air Baggage Allowance: 10 kg Carry-On, Checked 23 kg

‹ All rulesBaggageLiquidsPower bankVape
PerfumeAlcohol

Korean Air Economy and Premium Economy passengers can carry one carry-on bag plus one personal item, weighing no more than 10 kg in total. The carry-on is within 115 cm in total or 55 x 40 x 20 cm, and the personal item up to 40 x 30 x 15 cm. Free checked baggage on international flights (excluding the U.S. and Brazil) is 1 bag of up to 23 kg in Economy and Premium, 2 bags of up to 32 kg in Prestige and 3 bags of up to 32 kg in First. On domestic flights in Korea Economy is up to 20 kg.

Korean Air lists the allowance by booking class.

  • Personal item examples: a handbag, briefcase or laptop bag. Carry-on examples: a carry-on suitcase, backpack or Boston bag.
  • Heavy bags: a carry-on too heavy to store in the overhead bin should be checked in. Even a bag within the limits may be processed as checked baggage at the gate because of a lack of storage space.
  • Duty-free: duty-free items that exceed the carry-on allowance must be checked in at the boarding gate, and an excess baggage fee may apply.
  • Codeshare flights: the allowance may vary by operating airline.

  • Infants: on international flights 1 baggage + 1 foldable stroller + 1 car seat or bassinet; on domestic flights 1 foldable stroller + 1 car seat or bassinet. Children get the same allowance as adults plus a stroller and car seat.
  • Limits to know: some countries restrict a single piece over 32 kg (70 lb) or 158 cm (62 in) in total linear dimensions, whether or not you pay excess fees.

❓ Quick answers

What is the Korean Air baggage allowance?

Economy carry-on: one bag plus a personal item, 10 kg in total. Checked: 1 x 23 kg internationally in Economy, 2 x 32 kg in Prestige, 20 kg in Economy on Korean domestic flights.

What is the Korean Air carry-on size and weight?

Within 115 cm in total or 55 x 40 x 20 cm including handles and wheels, with a 40 x 30 x 15 cm personal item, and 10 kg in total in Economy.

How many checked bags does Korean Air Economy include?

1 bag of up to 23 kg on international flights excluding the U.S. and Brazil, and 2 bags of 23 kg for Economy above Saver and Premium to and from the Americas excluding Brazil.

What is the Korean Air checked baggage weight limit?

Economy bags are up to 23 kg, Prestige and First bags up to 32 kg; some countries restrict single pieces over 32 kg or 158 cm.

Does Korean Air allow more baggage in Prestige?

Yes: 2 bags up to 32 kg each in Prestige and 3 bags up to 32 kg in First, plus two carry-on bags up to 18 kg in total.

How much domestic baggage does Korean Air allow?

Economy 20 kg or less and Prestige 30 kg or less on domestic flights in Korea.

Checked 7 October 2026 on the airline's own pages. Baggage rules change by route, fare and date: the allowance on your ticket is the one that applies.
