Air China baggage allowance (2026): cabin & checked

Check

Air China economy: 1 cabin bag up to 5 kg (55 × 40 × 20 cm) + 1 personal item, per passenger.

Cabin and checked baggage by fare class. Fees are shown as ranges — always confirm the exact figure with Air China.

Source: Air China official baggage policy

Economy

Cabin: 1 cabin bag up to 5 kg (55 × 40 × 20 cm) + 1 personal item, per passenger.

Checked: International: 1 × 23 kg. Domestic China: 20 kg (weight). US routes: 2 × 23 kg (piece). Max 32 kg per bag.

Business

Cabin: 2 cabin bags + personal item, per passenger.

Checked: 2 checked bags up to 32 kg each, per passenger.
