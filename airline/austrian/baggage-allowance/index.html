Austrian baggage allowance (2026): cabin & checked

Check

Austrian economy: 1 cabin bag up to 8 kg (55 × 40 × 23 cm) + 1 personal item (40 × 30 × 15 cm), per passenger. Economy Basic on European routes: personal item only.

Cabin and checked baggage by fare class. Fees are shown as ranges — always confirm the exact figure with Austrian.

Source: Austrian official baggage policy

Economy (Basic / Light / Classic / Flex)

Cabin: 1 cabin bag up to 8 kg (55 × 40 × 23 cm) + 1 personal item (40 × 30 × 15 cm), per passenger. Economy Basic on European routes: personal item only.

Checked: Basic/Light: no checked bag — add for a fee. Classic and up: 1 × 23 kg. Max 32 kg / 158 cm per bag.

Business

Cabin: 2 cabin bags up to 8 kg each + personal item, per passenger.

Checked: 2 checked bags up to 32 kg each, per passenger.
