🎒 Virgin Australia carry-on baggage allowance

Virgin Australia says carry-on allowances vary by cabin class and Velocity membership status. Velocity Red and Silver members receive the standard Economy allowance.

WhoCarry-onPersonal item
Economy, Velocity Red and SilverOne standard bag, up to 8 kg, L56 x W36 x H23 cm1 item, L45 x W33 x H20 cm, fits under the seat in front
Business, Economy X, Velocity Gold, Platinum and Platinum PlusOne option, up to 14 kg combined, no single item over 8 kg1 item, L45 x W33 x H20 cm
  • The three options for the 14 kg allowance: one standard bag (8 kg, 56 x 36 x 23 cm); two small bags (14 kg combined, each up to 48 x 34 x 23 cm); or one standard bag plus one suit pack (14 kg combined, suit pack up to 114 x 60 x 11 cm).
  • Personal item examples on Virgin Australia's page: purse or handbag, laptop or camera bag, small backpack, reading material.
  • Partner-operated flights: guests on flights operated by Alliance Airlines or Link Airways on behalf of Virgin Australia receive the Economy carry-on allowance whatever their cabin or Velocity status. Charter guests receive the Economy allowance only.
  • Musical instruments up to L85 x W34 x H23 cm count as one carry-on bag; a cello or guitar that is larger needs an extra seat booked through the Guest Contact Centre.
  • Full lockers: if the overhead lockers are full, the team may ask you to check your carry-on at the gate. A bag over the size or weight limit cannot go in the cabin and must be checked, and baggage fees may apply.

This is the table for the Domestic and International Short Haul network. Velocity allowances depend on your status at the time of travel, not at booking.

  • Size and weight: each bag must not exceed 140 cm (55 in) in length + width + height, and no single item can weigh more than 32 kg. Heavier items must go as freight.
  • Infants: an infant not occupying a seat gets 3 special infant items; an infant with their own seat gets 3 items, plus 1 x 23 kg on every fare except Lite. A pram, portable cot, car seat and baby capsule are examples of infant items.
  • Wheelchairs and mobility aids for personal use are always carried free of charge.
  • Partner flights and codeshares: Velocity Silver, Gold and Platinum extras vary with the operating carrier; for Virgin Australia codeshare bookings, Virgin Australia refers you to the non-member allowance.

💲 Virgin Australia extra and overweight bag fees

Prices are per piece (maximum 23 kg) and per sector, in the booking currency. Virgin Australia publishes a range, and the price you pay depends on flight length, travel date and when and where you buy.

Domestic extra bag (per bag)Price range
Pre-purchase before the airport (current)$67 to $102
Pre-purchase for bookings made on or after 28 October 2026$77 to $107
At the airport$120 to $170
  • How many you can add: an additional two bags may be purchased online or through the Guest Contact Centre.
  • Overweight: a bag over 23 kg and up to 32 kg is charged at the airport, $65 per piece on domestic flights, and AUD 110 (or NZD 110 from New Zealand) on international short haul flights, per piece. Anything over 32 kg goes as freight.
  • International extra bags: Virgin Australia publishes separate prices by departure country on its checked baggage page, and may change its fees at any time. We did not copy them here.

❓ Quick answers

What is the Virgin Australia baggage allowance?

Economy: one carry-on bag up to 8 kg (56 x 36 x 23 cm) plus one personal item (45 x 33 x 20 cm). Checked baggage by fare: Lite none, Choice and Flex 1 x 23 kg, Business 2 x 32 kg, with extra bags for Velocity members.

What is the Virgin Australia carry-on size and weight?

The standard bag is up to 8 kg and L56 x W36 x H23 cm; the personal item is L45 x W33 x H20 cm and must fit under the seat in front. Business, Economy X and Gold, Platinum and Platinum Plus members can take up to 14 kg combined, no single bag over 8 kg.

Does Virgin Australia Lite include checked baggage?

No. Lite includes no checked baggage for anyone, including Velocity Red, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Platinum Plus members. Choice and Flex include 1 x 23 kg for non-members and Red members.

What is the Virgin Australia checked baggage weight limit?

No single item can weigh more than 32 kg, and each bag must be no more than 140 cm in length + width + height. A bag over 23 kg and up to 32 kg pays an overweight fee at the airport; $65 per piece domestically.

How much is an extra bag on Virgin Australia domestic flights?

Virgin Australia publishes a range: $67 to $102 to pre-purchase before the airport and $120 to $170 at the airport, per bag per sector. From 28 October 2026 the pre-purchase range is $77 to $107.

Can Velocity members carry more on Virgin Australia?

Gold, Platinum and Platinum Plus members get the 14 kg carry-on allowance; Red and Silver members receive the standard Economy allowance. For checked bags on Choice and Flex, Silver and Gold get 2 x 23 kg, Platinum 3 x 23 kg and Platinum Plus 5 x 32 kg.

Checked 7 October 2026 on the airline's own pages. Baggage rules change by route, fare and date: the allowance on your ticket is the one that applies.
