🎒 Hand baggage

ItemMaximum
Hand bag10 kg / 22 lb, 55 × 40 × 25 cm / 22 × 16 × 10 in
Personal item (under the seat)2 kg / 4.4 lb, 40 × 30 × 15 cm / 16 × 12 × 5 in
  • Liquids go in individual containers of up to 100 ml in one transparent bag of up to 1 liter.
  • Special cabin baggage (for example an instrument) needs an extra seat booked when you book or at least 72 hours before the flight.

❓ Quick answers

What is the TAP Air Portugal hand baggage size?

55 × 40 × 25 cm and up to 10 kg, plus a personal item of 40 × 30 × 15 cm and up to 2 kg.

What is the TAP checked bag weight limit?

23 kg (50 lb) in Economy and 32 kg (70 lb) in Business, within 158 cm. Baggage over 32 kg must be transported as cargo.

How much is an extra bag on TAP?

From 30 EUR / 37 USD / 49 CAD per extra piece when bought online in advance; heavy and oversize bags are priced by route on TAP's hold baggage page.

Checked 7 October 2026 on the airline's own pages. Baggage rules change by route, fare and date: the allowance on your ticket is the one that applies.
