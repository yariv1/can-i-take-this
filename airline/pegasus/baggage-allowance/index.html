Light (international / North Cyprus) Economy PER PASSENGER

On the plain

1 under-seat bag only, up to 40 × 30 × 15 cm and 3 kg — no overhead cabin bag. Add cabin baggage or a higher fare for the 55 × 40 × 23 cm / 8 kg trolley.

Checked in

No checked bag included — add one for a fee (confirm on flypgs.com). Max 32 kg.

Saver Economy PER PASSENGER

On the plain

1 cabin bag (55 × 40 × 23 cm, 8 kg) plus 1 under-seat bag.

Checked in

Checked bag included — roughly 15 kg domestic, 20 kg international.

Saver Plus / Comfort Flex Economy PER PASSENGER

On the plain

1 cabin bag plus 1 under-seat bag.

Checked in

20 kg or more included, up to 32 kg per bag.

Rules change and vary by nationality, route and fare. This is guidance, not legal advice — always confirm with the airline or the official customs authority before you travel. Updated 2026.
