El Al baggage allowance (2026): cabin & checked

Check

El Al economy: 1 carry-on bag up to 8 kg (max 56 × 45 × 25 cm) per passenger, plus 1 small personal item like a backpack or laptop bag.

Cabin and checked baggage by fare class. Fees are shown as ranges — always confirm the exact figure with El Al.

Source: El Al official baggage policy

Economy (Classic & Flex)

Cabin: 1 carry-on bag up to 8 kg (max 56 × 45 × 25 cm) per passenger, plus 1 small personal item like a backpack or laptop bag.

Checked: 1–2 checked bags, 23 kg each, per passenger — depends on the exact fare and route.

Economy (Lite)

Cabin: Per passenger: standard cabin suitcases may be checked free at the gate due to fare rules; you can still bring 1 small personal item into the cabin.

Checked: No checked bag included per passenger — add one for a fee.

Premium Class

Cabin: 2 carry-on bags per passenger, max 8 kg each (combined up to 12 kg).

Checked: 2 checked bags per passenger, 23 kg each.

Business Class

Cabin: 2 carry-on bags per passenger, max 16 kg each (combined up to 20 kg).

Checked: 2 checked bags per passenger, 32 kg each.
