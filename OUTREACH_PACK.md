## 1. The numbers (counted from the live table, 33 airlines, page dated 1 Oct 2026)
- **23 of 33** airlines ban using or charging a power bank on board.
- **5** limit it (for example only outside taxi, take-off and landing, or no charging the bank itself): Ryanair, Qatar Airways, ANA, Air New Zealand, Delta.
- **3** have no in-flight ban in the rule we found: British Airways, Pegasus, American Airlines.
- **2** publish no in-flight rule in the pages we read: Icelandair, Air Canada.
- **3** allow only one power bank per passenger: Wizz Air, Emirates, Etihad.
- Every row links the airline's own page as the source.
- Caveat to always state: rules change; the table shows the date each rule started where the airline published one.
