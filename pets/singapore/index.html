Can I bring a dog into Singapore? (2026 pet import)

With limits

Singapore — plan months ahead for a dog.

Pet import into Singapore is time-sensitive — some steps (microchip, rabies titre test, permits) take months. Start early.

  • Singapore is rabies-controlled: a dog needs a microchip, rabies vaccination, a blood-titre test, an import permit and possibly quarantine.
  • Some countries ban or restrict specific breeds (e.g. certain "dangerous dog" types) — check the destination's breed list.
